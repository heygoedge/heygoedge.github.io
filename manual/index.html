01

Heygo 是什么

Heygo 由两个传感器和 Heygo App 组成。传感器夹在两只雪鞋外侧，实时捕捉你滑行时的姿态和动作；App 里的模型和 AI 教练把这些数据变成你能听懂的反馈。它不只是记录你滑了多快多远，而是看懂你怎么滑。

一个持续陪伴的教练循环

看见

捕捉你自己感觉不到的细节：重心变化、施压、时机、节奏和弯型。

理解

把细节连成背后的规律：哪里变了、什么起效了、什么在拖你后腿。

提醒

在最关键的时候给一句提示，让你在坏习惯重复之前改过来。

记住

每一趟都会丰富你的滑手档案，滑得越多，它越懂你下一步该练什么。

L · R

04

上手 6 步

  1. 先充电

    把两个传感器放进充电座并接上电源。配对和固件升级都需要它们在座上，所以从这一步开始。

  2. 下载 App 并登录

    在 App Store 或 Google Play 下载 Heygo。iOS 可用 Apple、Google 或邮箱登录；Android 可用 Google 或邮箱登录。

  3. 扫包装盒上的码

    包装上的二维码就是这套设备的配对码，会把设备绑定到你的账号，盒子请留好。

  4. 连接并升级固件

    传感器放在座上，点「连接」，然后马上做一次固件升级：在家几分钟，在山上差不多半趟缆车。

  5. 设置站姿

    填好名字、站姿、水平等基础信息。Regular（左脚在前）还是 Goofy（右脚在前）一定要选对，前刃 / 后刃的判断都依赖它。

  6. 夹上就滑

    L 夹左脚、R 夹右脚，夹在雪鞋外侧。打开 App，在 Go 页面滑动开始，手机放回口袋就可以滑了。最初几个弯是 Heygo 在自动校准，大约第四个弯就会听到第一条教练语音。

使用小贴士

  • 夹在雪鞋外侧，夹紧不晃。设备松动会影响角度准确性；鞋壳特别薄或特别厚时，建议加固定绑带。
  • L / R 别装反：左右脚装反会让前后刃判断全部颠倒。
  • 手机放在身上（口袋里即可），和设备保持在蓝牙范围内。
  • 想听实时语音就戴上任意耳机；不想被打扰可以选「静音」模式，滑完再看报告。
  • 山上信号差也没关系：核心功能离线可用，联网后报告会补上。
  • 每晚放回充电座充电；低温下耗电更快，留意低电量提醒。

常见问题

Heygo 能用于双板吗？

目前 Heygo 专为单板滑雪设计和开发，暂不支持双板。

所有雪鞋都能夹吗？

夹子式设计适配大多数主流单板雪鞋，但不保证 100% 适配。鞋壳特别薄或特别厚时可能夹不稳，建议加固定绑带。

和运动手表有什么区别？

手表主要记录心率、速度、距离和路线。Heygo 关注的是你的身体和雪板怎么动：立刃、施压、时机、弯型，告诉你「怎么滑」，而不只是「滑了多快多远」。

没网能用吗？

可以。核心功能在弱网或无网时仍可用，报告会在恢复联网后补上。

充一次电能用多久？

最长约 7 天。建议每晚放回充电座，低温环境下耗电会更快。

完全不会滑能用吗？

Heygo 以「弯」为单位分析技术，所以能连续换刃之后才真正发挥作用。还在学站立、推坡的阶段，它能记录滑行和摔倒，但还分析不出技术问题。

What is Heygo

Heygo is two sensors plus the Heygo app. The sensors clip to the outside of your boots and capture how you move as you ride; the models and AI coach in the app turn that into feedback you can use. It doesn't just log how fast or far you went. It understands how you ride.

A coaching loop that keeps going

Notices

Captures what you can't feel in the moment: balance shifts, pressure, timing, rhythm and turn shape.

Understands

Connects the details to the bigger pattern: what changed, what worked, what's holding you back.

Responds

One focused cue at the right moment, before the habit repeats.

Remembers

Every run adds to your rider profile. The more you ride, the better it knows what's next.

Get started in 6 steps

  1. Charge both sensors

    Put both sensors in the dock and plug it in. Pairing and firmware updates both need them docked, so start here.

  2. Get the app and sign in

    Download Heygo from the App Store or Google Play. iOS: sign in with Apple, Google or email. Android: Google or email.

  3. Scan the code on the box

    The QR code on the box is your Pairing Code. It ties this set to your account, so keep the box.

  4. Connect and update

    With both sensors docked, tap Connect and run the firmware update right away: a few minutes at home, half a chairlift on the mountain.

  5. Set your stance

    Tell Heygo your name, stance, level and a few basics. Get Regular vs Goofy right: every toeside/heelside call depends on it.

  6. Clip on and ride

    L on your left boot, R on your right, on the outside of each boot. Open the app, slide to start on Go, phone back in your pocket. The first few turns calibrate automatically; around the fourth turn you'll hear your first cue.

Tips

  • Clip on the outside of each boot, snug and secure. A loose sensor hurts angle accuracy; for very thin or thick boot shells, add the securing strap.
  • Don't swap L and R. Swapping them flips every toeside/heelside call.
  • Keep your phone on you (a pocket is fine), within Bluetooth range of the sensors.
  • Wear any earbuds for real-time voice, or choose Silent mode and read the reports later.
  • Weak signal on the mountain? Core functions work offline; reports catch up once you're connected.
  • Dock them every night. Cold drains the battery faster, so watch for low-battery alerts.

FAQ

Can I use Heygo for skiing?

Heygo is currently designed specifically for snowboarding, not skiing.

Does it fit every boot?

The clip fits most standard snowboard boots, but not 100%. Very thin or thick shells may be less secure; use the securing strap.

How is it different from a sports watch?

Watches track heart rate, speed, distance and route. Heygo tracks how your body and board move (edging, pressure, timing, turn shape), so you learn how you ride, not just how fast or far.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Core functions work with little or no connection; reports appear once you're back online.

How long does a charge last?

Up to 7 days. Dock it every night; cold weather drains the battery faster.

I've never ridden. Is it for me?

Heygo analyzes turn by turn, so it really shines once you can link turns. Before that, it can log your rides and falls, but there's no technique to analyze yet.
