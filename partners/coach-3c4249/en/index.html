ABOUT HEYGO

What is Heygo

Heygo is an AI wearable built for snowboarding. Clip it onto the side of your boots and it breaks down every turn you ride: how that run went, where you leveled up, and what to work on next lap.

The hardest part of progressing is knowing exactly what to fix. Heygo turns every run into clear data, a quick breakdown and drills to try, plus real-time voice cues while you ride. Riders train with purpose, coaches teach with evidence, and clubs and resorts get a fresh tech experience to offer.
